# A Portal Home

> A handcrafted, human-made game.

---

## About

Escape a haunted retro-futuristic spaceship by fixing a broken teleport. A portal will open and bring you back home, well… hopefully. 

You can collect cassette recordings which after being played will tell you how to repair the teleporter step by step. Read them carefully!

---

## Important Notes

* **Cursor Visibility:** If your mouse cursor ends up visible because of notifications or ALT-tabbing, you can fix it by pausing the game (`TAB`).
* **Initial Startup:** The first startup may lag or freeze briefly after the intro—bear with it for a few seconds and then it’s fine :)) (likely due to material/light loading).

---

## Hints

* **Cassette Order:** The cassettes are meant to be inserted and played in order from **1 to 8**. If you are missing earlier ones (e.g., #1), you need to find #1 first. To insert cassettes into the player, click on the cassette window and the sequential ones will be automatically inserted.
* **Success Feedback:** Repairing any part of the teleporter plays a short success cue sound.
* **Cable Connections:** Click on one end and then the other to connect cables. To disconnect them, **right-click on one of the ends** (not the cable itself).

> **SPOILER WARNING:** When the monster comes after some time lurking, hide in the closet by dragging the door open and then physically walking into it.
